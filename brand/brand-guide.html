Brand guide for commercial roof coatings across the Bay Area and Central Valley, since 1985. Use it for proposals, trucks, shirts, yard signs, emails and social posts, so every touchpoint looks like the same trusted company.

The mark

A roof line over a drop of coating: the roof you already own, renewed. Use the horizontal logo where there's room, and the square mark for profile pictures, hard hats and favicons. Keep clear space around it equal to the height of the drop.

How we sound

Plain, confident, specific. Talk like the person who walked the roof, not like an ad. Every claim should have a number, a photo or a warranty behind it.

Proof points to repeat everywhere

  • Free roof check-up with a written condition report, photos and moisture check
  • Three priced options in writing, with the renewal vs. replacement cost shown side by side
  • Manufacturer warranties of 10, 15 or 20 years, plus our own workmanship warranty
  • Licensed (CSLB #, always shown), insured, with a written safety plan on every roof
  • Yearly check-up plans that keep the warranty in good standing

Do and don't

Do

  • Use real photos of your own roofs and crews: before, during, after.
  • Show the CSLB license number on every bid, truck and ad (California law).
  • Lead with the owner's money: cost per sq ft, savings vs. replacement, warranty years.
  • Keep layouts mostly white, with blue for structure and green for the one thing that matters.

Don't

  • Stretch, recolor or add effects to the logo.
  • Put green text on blue (hard to read). Use white on blue.
  • Use stock photos of houses or shingle roofs. We do commercial flat roofs.
  • Promise tax savings or rebates. Point owners to their CPA and the program's official page.
