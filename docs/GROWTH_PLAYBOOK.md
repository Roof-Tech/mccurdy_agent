# Growth playbook

A weekly system that turns the toolkit into booked roofs. Growth comes from doing this every week, not from any single tool.

## The weekly rhythm
| Day | Do | Tool |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Pull or refresh 50 prospects in one city; mark the top 20 | `prospects score` / `prospects map` |
| Mon | Look up owner/manager contacts for the top 20 (assessor owner + LinkedIn / company site) | prospect sheet "Notes" |
| Tue | Load the top 20 into a follow-up sequence; review drafts, send step 1 | `followups` + Gmail |
| Tue–Thu | 10 calls a day from the sheet; 2 walk-in business parks | call scripts |
| Wed–Fri | Roof check-ups booked from calls/emails; core samples + photos | phone camera, moisture meter |
| Within 48 h of check-up | Proposal out, with the three options and renewal vs. replacement | `proposal` |
| 3 days after proposal | Call to walk through it; answer questions | proposal portal shows if they opened it |
| Friday | One social post; one review request; update the prospect sheet statuses | social + review templates |

## Numbers to track (one row per week)
Prospects added · first contacts · check-ups done · proposals sent · proposals signed · $ signed · average $/sq ft · yearly care agreements signed.

If check-ups are low, make more calls. If proposals don't close, change the offer (warranty tiers, financing, payment schedule). If the price per sq ft is low, raise it on the Good tier first.

## Where bigger revenue comes from
1. **Bigger roofs**: filter prospects to 50k+ sq ft warehouses, cold storage and packing houses. One 100k sq ft roof can equal ten small ones.
2. **Repeat channels**: property-management firms and commercial brokers send many buildings, not one. Sign up two or three of them.
3. **Yearly care agreements** on every finished roof: steady income in the slow season, and the re-coat job in 10–15 years.
4. **Manufacturer certification** (GAF, Gaco, Tremco, Henry or similar) unlocks NDL warranties that large owners and lenders ask for.
5. **Proof**: one case study per month, with photos and the savings figure.

Be realistic about growth: 5–10x in a year is possible only from a small base, with the crew, insurance limits and bonding capacity to match. Hire and get bonded ahead of the bids you plan to win.
