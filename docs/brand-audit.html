Brand audit and the first-page answers

What commercial owners, managers, ag operators and brokers search for and expect, how the McCurdy crest holds up, and exactly what to put on page one to win the estimate request.

The verdict

Keep the crest and colors. Fix how it’s used, and lead with proof.

The crest is the right foundation: a shield for protection, a low-slope ridge that says “commercial,” a classic M that says “established,” and three bars that tell the three-coat story. It does not need a redesign. It needs a complete system of versions, one locked navy, and a first page that answers the six things every commercial buyer checks before they call.

The biggest single opportunity is not the logo. Competitors across California already advertise 30-year NDL warranties. What none of the NorCal competitors I found show is a published, step-by-step install standard with rep inspections on every job, mil readings delivered with the warranty, and three years of free maintenance. That process is McCurdy’s moat. Put it on page one.

Logo audit

How the crest performs where it actually appears

A logo for a commercial contractor lives on truck doors at 60 mph, hard-hat stickers, embroidered polos, Google Maps pins at 24 pixels, and the corner of a PDF. I tested the crest at every size and on every background.

Keep
  • The shield, ridge, M and three coat bars
  • Navy, green, white and black
  • The Cinzel wordmark: classical capitals read as heritage, which fits “since 1985”
  • Lettering converted to outlines, so any sign shop reproduces it exactly
Added today
  • Small mark (shield + M only) for favicons, map pins and stickers under 1 inch
  • Reversed crest (green shield) for navy shirts, trucks and dark slides
  • One-color crest and logo for embroidery, vinyl cutting, stamps and fax-quality print
  • Compact logo without the tagline for business cards, shirt chests and email signatures
Change
  • Lock one navy. The crest uses #0E2A47, the older brand sheet #0F3D6E and the app a third blue. Pick #0E2A47 everywhere so trucks, shirts and screens match.
  • Descriptor line: “Commercial Roof Renewal” is not what buyers type. Use “Commercial Roof Restoration & Waterproofing,” which matches real searches and your full service list.
  • Minimum sizes: full crest 48 px or 0.5 in; below that, use the small mark. Tagline only when the logo is 2 in wide or more.
Remove
  • “The Gold Standard” on the website. There is no gold in the brand, and the phrase is common in the trade. Replace it with “The McCurdy Standard,” which you can prove step by step.
  • Mixed numbers on the website (20+ cities, 40 cities, 10,094+ services). Use one verified figure set everywhere.
  • “First and only” claims. Southern California contractors advertise 30-year NDL too, so it can be disproven.

Proposed upgrades to the identity system

  • Endorsement line: “A Mike McCurdy Roofing company · Since 1985” under the logo on proposals and the website footer. Forty years of a family name builds trust faster than any symbol.
  • Credential bar: a fixed row of the manufacturer approvals (ASC, SOPREMA, APOC), CSLB #477152, and “Insured · Bonded” placed the same way on every page, proposal and truck.
  • Truck and site kit: navy truck with the reversed crest, phone number and CSLB number at 4 in tall; white hard hats with the small mark; an 18 × 24 in job sign with “Roof restoration in progress” and a QR code to book an assessment.
  • Photo style: real crews with fall protection, mil gauges, chalk-line grids and finished white roofs from a drone. No stock photos of houses or shingles.

What buyers expect

Each buyer checks different things before they ask for an estimate

Owners and investors

  • Total cost over the roof’s life, not the lowest bid
  • A written inspection report before any price
  • A manufacturer NDL warranty: non-prorated, labor and materials, and whether it transfers when they sell
  • A certified applicator for that exact system
  • References on buildings like theirs, and online reviews

Your answer: free assessment with core samples; up to 30-year NDL from three manufacturers; the comparison table; three case studies.

Property and facility managers

  • Certificate of insurance naming the owner and manager as additional insured (not just certificate holder), usually $1M / $2M general liability plus workers’ comp
  • W-9, license, safety record
  • No tenant disruption: noise, access, loading docks
  • Closeout package: roof plan, photo log, punch list
  • Maintenance records that keep the warranty valid

Your answer: a one-click vendor packet; the roof record app; free maintenance for 3 years.

Ag, food processing and cold storage

  • No shutdown: work around 24/7 operations and harvest season
  • Low odor near processing and packing lines
  • Protection for corrosion-prone metal roofs and decks
  • Cooler roofs for refrigeration and packing houses

Your answer: a dedicated ag and food page with an off-hours schedule and product data sheets on request.

Realtors and commercial brokers

  • A fast roof condition report during escrow
  • Remaining useful life in years, which lenders and buyers underwrite against
  • A written price to fix it, so the roof stops being a deal risk
  • A warranty that can transfer to the buyer

Your answer: an “Escrow Roof Report” product with a set turnaround and remaining-life estimate.

The plan

What moves McCurdy toward a top-3 spot

Rank is earned by reviews, relevant pages and proof, not by design alone. In order of payoff:

  1. Google Business Profile: crest as the photo, the reversed crest as cover, weekly before/after posts, and a review request after every job. Reviews are one of the top things business buyers check.
  2. Vendor packet page: COI sample, W-9, license, safety program and manufacturer letters, downloadable without a phone call.
  3. Three case studies with drone photos, mil readings and the warranty term: one warehouse, one ag or food building, one office or retail center.
  4. Audience pages: Owners & Investors, Property Managers, Ag & Food Processing, Realtors & Brokers, each answering its list above.
  5. Escrow Roof Report: a named product for brokers with a set turnaround and remaining-life estimate.
  6. One navy, everywhere: website, app, proposals, trucks and shirts.
Before anything goes public: confirm APOC’s warranty length from its letter, fill in your real Google rating, and check that “Insured & bonded” and your insurance limits match your current certificates.
