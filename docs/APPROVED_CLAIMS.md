# Approved marketing claims

Use only these claims in proposals, emails, the presentation, the website and ads. Each one has a source.
If a claim isn't here, check with the owner before using it.

| Claim | Wording to use | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Warranty length | Full-system NDL warranties up to 30 years | Written approvals from ASC, SOPREMA (formerly Tropical Roofing Products) and APOC, on file |
| Warranty scope | Labor and materials, day one through the final year; no upcharge for the warranty | Manufacturer warranty documents |
| Warranty delivery | Delivered in writing within 30 days of completion | McCurdy policy |
| vs. other roof types | Up to 10 years longer than a typical TPO, PVC or modified-bitumen roof warranty | Owner's comparison of manufacturer warranty terms |
| Warranty years vs. a new roof | Up to 50% more warranted years (30-year NDL vs. a typical 20-year TPO, PVC or modified-bitumen warranty) | Owner's comparison of manufacturer warranty terms |
| Price | Up to 45% less than a full replacement by a commercial roofing company (lead with warranty years; price second) | McCurdy project history |
| Why low bids can't carry the warranty | A 30-year NDL system needs 4–5.5 gallons of primer and silicone per 100 sq ft; skip one required step or come in under thickness and the manufacturer can refuse the warranty | Manufacturer specifications and McCurdy method |
| Thickness | 5–10 mils over spec on standard renewals; 10–50% over required thickness on every 20 to 30-year NDL project to date | McCurdy mil-reading records |
| Inspections | Manufacturer representative inspects every commercial job | McCurdy policy |
| Aftercare | Free inspection and maintenance every year for the first 3 years | McCurdy policy |
| Industry problem | Our manufacturer reps estimate as many as 95% of coating installations they inspect skip steps, run under spec, and carry no manufacturer warranty | Attributed to reps; always say "our reps estimate" |
| History | Since 1985 · CSLB #477152 | CSLB record, website |

## Do not use without written proof
- "First and only" / "the only company in the USA" to offer full NDL coverage
- "The longest commercial roof warranty on the market"
- Any savings percentage other than "up to 45%" (no "40–70%" or "60% cheaper")
- Naming a competitor, or calling any company a scam, fraud or "bad actor", or saying they "can't legally" offer a warranty. Teach the buyer the math and the questions instead; the buyer draws the conclusion
- Price comparisons with sole proprietors
- Any rebate or tax credit not listed on the app's Savings & Tax Info page
