# Reviews and case studies

## Review request (text or email, send the day after final walk-through)
> Hi [first name], thank you for choosing McCurdy Coatings for [property]. If you're happy with the work, a short Google review would mean a lot to our small team: [Google review link]
> Even one sentence about the crew or the process helps other owners find us. Thank you! — [your name]

Follow up once after 5 days if there's no review. Never offer payment or discounts for reviews (it breaks Google's rules and FTC guidance).

## Case study template (one page, for the website, proposals and LinkedIn)
**Title:** [Building type], [City]: [sq ft] sq ft renewed in [N] days
**The problem:** [Leaks? Ponding? Age? What the owner was worried about.]
**What we found:** [Core samples, condition, 2–3 photos]
**What we did:** [System, dry mils, details like drains/curbs]
**Result:**
- Cost vs. replacement: $[renewal] vs. about $[replacement] → saved [X]%
- Warranty: [N]-year manufacturer + [N]-year workmanship
- Building stayed open: [yes / tenants unaffected]
- Roof surface temperature: [before °F → after °F, if measured with an IR thermometer]
**Owner quote:** "[Ask permission to use it.]" — [Name, title, company]
**Photos:** before · during · after (same angle)

Get written permission before using an owner's name, building photos or quote.
