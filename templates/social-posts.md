# Weekly posts (Google Business Profile, LinkedIn, Facebook)

Post once a week. Real photos only. Each post: one photo, 2–4 sentences, one call to action.

1. **Before/after** — "Before and after on a [sq ft] sq ft warehouse in [city]. Silicone renewal over the existing roof, no tear-off, building open the whole time. Free roof check-ups: [phone]."
2. **What we found** — Photo of ponding / cracked seam. "This is what 18 Valley summers do to a flat roof. Caught early, it's a renewal, not a replacement."
3. **Crew & safety** — Crew with fall protection set up. "Safety plan on every roof, every day. Proud of this crew."
4. **The numbers** — Simple graphic: replacement $ vs. renewal $. "Same building, two options."
5. **Mil gauge** — Close-up of the wet-mil gauge. "We measure the coating thickness across the whole roof. That's what your warranty is based on."
6. **Customer review** — Screenshot (with permission). "Thank you, [company]!"
7. **Seasonal** — Before rain season: "Clear your drains before the first storm. We do yearly check-ups."
8. **Heat** — IR thermometer on old vs. coated roof. "[X]°F cooler on the same afternoon."
