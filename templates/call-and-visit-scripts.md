# Phone and walk-in scripts

Keep it short, polite and specific. The goal of every first contact is one thing: **book a free roof check-up**.

## Cold call — owner or property manager (60 seconds)
> Hi, is this [name]? This is [your name] with McCurdy Coatings. We renew commercial flat roofs here in the Valley.
> I'm calling about the building at [address]. Do you have a minute?
>
> We do free roof check-ups. We walk the roof, take core samples to see if the insulation's still dry, and send you a written report with photos. You keep it either way.
> If the roof's still sound underneath, a silicone renewal usually costs 40 to 70 percent less than a tear-off, and your tenants keep working.
>
> Could I get on the roof [Tuesday or Thursday] this week?

**"We're not looking right now."** → Totally fair. Most owners use our report to plan a budget a year or two out. Want it on file so you have real numbers when you need them?
**"We already have a roofer."** → Great. Do they do restoration coatings or mostly replacements? A second opinion in writing costs you nothing.
**"Send me something."** → Happy to. What's the best email? I'll send a sample report and follow up [day].
**"How much?"** → It depends on the roof's condition, which is why the check-up is free. For a [size] roof, renewal typically runs [range from your price sheet].

## Walk-in at a business park
Ask for the manager or the owner's contact. Leave the one-page leave-behind with your card. Write down the building number, roof type you can see from the ground (parapets, HVAC units, ponding stains on walls), and who you talked to. Log it in the prospect sheet the same day.

## Realtor intro (coffee or call)
> When a roof comes up in escrow, I can get a photo condition report and a written renewal price to you within 3 business days. That gives your buyer a number instead of a scare. Can I be your roof contact for commercial listings?

## Voicemail (20 seconds)
> Hi [name], [your name] with McCurdy Coatings, about the roof at [address]. We do free commercial roof check-ups with a written photo report. I'll send you an email too. My number is [phone]. Again, [phone]. Thanks.
